BEST TIME TO VISIT

Cultural Calendars & Festivals

Plan around significant cultural moments, favorable seasonal weather, and peaceful crowd windows.

Winter (Oct – Mar)

Peak Comfort

Ideal for North India (Varanasi, Jaipur, Amritsar) and Karnataka boulder trails in Hampi. Crisp mornings, clear sunshine, and pleasant walking weather.

Top Events: Dev Deepawali, Jaipur Lit Fest

Post-Monsoon (Aug – Oct)

Vibrant Culture

Lush green landscapes across Udaipur's lakes and Kerala backwaters. Extraordinary festival atmosphere during autumn Durga Puja in Kolkata.

Top Events: Kolkata Durga Puja, Navratri

Spring (Feb – Apr)

Mild & Floral

Ideal for temple cities of Tamil Nadu (Madurai Chithirai festival) and sacred celebrations before peak summer heat sets in.

Top Events: Chithirai Festival, Maha Shivratri
